# On War — Carl von Clausewitz

**Original title:** *Vom Kriege*  
**Author:** Carl von Clausewitz (1780–1831)  
**First published:** 1832 (posthumously)  
**Subject:** Military theory, strategy, politics, and philosophy

## About the Work

*On War* examines the nature of war, the relationship between military action and political objectives, uncertainty in decision-making, and the forces that shape conflict. Clausewitz distinguishes war in theory from war as it is actually fought.

## Abbé's Library Edition

This directory is intended for a modern-English edition that makes Clausewitz's arguments more accessible without censoring the text, changing his conclusions, or presenting interpretation as translation.

**Editorial principles**
- Preserve the author's meaning, argument, and structure.
- Use clear contemporary English while retaining important technical distinctions.
- Keep original book and chapter references.
- Explain unfamiliar historical or military terms in clearly separated notes.
- Identify the source edition and translator before publishing adapted chapters.
